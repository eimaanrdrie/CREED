# BRD-COL-088 — Collections Promise-to-Pay Business Requirement
Product: Collections
Module: Promise-to-Pay

A Promise-to-Pay commitment must maintain a consistent lifecycle across accepted customer and upstream events. Repeated delivery of the same business event must not create an unintended additional commitment-state transition. Implementation controls may vary by client, so shared delivery-method reuse must remain traceable to client-specific configuration and test evidence.
